# StudyMind AI — 2-Minute Pitch

## Opening — Problem

Students have access to more educational content than ever, but more content does not automatically tell them what to study next. An AI tutor can explain a topic when asked. The harder problem is understanding what this particular learner is ready for, where they are struggling, and which gap matters next.

## Solution — What StudyMind AI does

StudyMind AI connects the structure of the material to the state of the learner. It processes study PDFs, represents concepts and prerequisite relationships in a knowledge graph, and tracks concept-level mastery from assessment activity. It uses that context to identify weaknesses, create targeted practice, and recommend a next action.

## How it works

**Material → Knowledge Graph → Learner Model → Diagnosis → Adaptive Practice → Mastery Update → Next Best Action**

The demo makes this loop visible. The learner begins with Trees at 42% mastery and Recursion at 51%. The map shows Recursion as a prerequisite for Trees. StudyMind AI uses that context to target practice. The backend scores the submitted answers, records the assessment, updates the learner model, and recalculates the recommendation.

In one recorded demo run, Trees changed from 42.0% to 53.0%, a change of 11.0 points, and the recommendation changed. Those are application-level values from that run, not guaranteed results or measured educational gains.

## Differentiation

StudyMind AI is more than an explainer or chat interface because the central experience is persistent learner state connected to concept relationships and assessment results. Its goal is not only to answer a prompt; it is to make a reasoned next-step recommendation from the current model. The current prototype uses a heuristic learner model and rule-based diagnosis, so it is an early demonstration of that product loop rather than validated learning science.

## Demo transition

“Let me show you that loop in the app. On the Dashboard, StudyMind AI identifies Trees as a weakness and recommends a next action. I’ll open the Knowledge Map to show the prerequisite, then run a short adaptive session and see how the recommendation responds.”

## Closing

“Most AI tutors answer what you ask. StudyMind AI figures out what you should learn next.”
