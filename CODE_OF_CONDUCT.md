# Code of Conduct

We want participation in the StudyMind AI community to be respectful and constructive.

Examples of behavior that supports this community include:

- Using welcoming, considerate language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and learners.

Harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, discriminatory behavior, and intentionally disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
