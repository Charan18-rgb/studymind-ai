# StudyMind AI — 30-Second Pitch

Most AI study tools can explain a topic. The harder problem is knowing what this learner should learn next. StudyMind AI builds a knowledge graph from study material, maintains a model of the learner, identifies prerequisite weaknesses, and adapts practice based on what the learner actually understands. When mastery changes, the recommendation changes too. StudyMind AI — Adaptive learning that understands the learner.
